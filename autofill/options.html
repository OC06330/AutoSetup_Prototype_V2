Settings

Settings for the four tools in this extension. Shared settings come first; then each tool has its own block. Everything here is yours alone: it follows your browser profile and changes nothing in Salesforce or Awin until you next use the tool.

Menu style on records

How the Checklist Autofill shows on Salesforce records. Its menu can also start the other tools. Changes apply straight away, in every open Salesforce tab.

Reading client websites

Lets the tools open a client's public website (from the record) to see what it runs on. They only read it, without your cookies, and close any tab they open. Your browser asks once.

  • Checklist Autofill: Auto-detect platform picks the platform's template from the Landing Page URL.
  • AutoSetup: a Shopify store's own myshopify address as the Website URL, and for Access, whether the query string is source=aw or utm_source=awin.
  • Internal Review: the site's eCommerce platform, and Shopify's internal domain for the accepted domains.

🌙 Moonfill

Runs Moonpull scans from the record and posts the result to Chatter.

Built by Rodier Sangibala

No settings of its own.

  • Follow your scans, and clear their history, from the extension's toolbar icon (History).
  • Needs access to the shared Moonpull results folder in SharePoint, and you signed in to Microsoft 365.
  • After a scan it fills five scan fields, three of which the checklist also has team defaults for: see Checklist Autofill to keep the scan's values.

📋 Checklist Autofill

Fills and saves the record's Technical Checklist.

Built by Oscar Coleman

Fields

Choose what each Technical Checklist field is filled with. Anything left on Team default follows the agreed team rules. For a dropdown, a value must match one of its options exactly as Salesforce shows it. If it doesn't, the run stops before saving and lists the options. Save with the bar at the bottom of the page.

🚀 AutoSetup

Sets up a new programme on the ten Awin UI pages from the record (🚀 New Implementation), then posts a summary to Chatter.

Built by Oscar Coleman

No settings of its own.

  • Uses Reading client websites for Shopify stores' myshopify address and the Access query string.
  • Checks you're signed in to Awin before each run. Every value comes from the record, or from the by-hand form when something is missing.
  • Its Chatter summary is what the Internal Review checks against.

🔎 Internal Review

Reviews the programme in the Awin UI against the setup's Chatter note, fixes what it may, deletes the test transactions, fills blank record fields and reports to Chatter (🔎 IR on the record). Shift-click 🔎 IR, or Just open the IR tabs in its window, to only open the tabs.

Built by Kaio Da Mota (Optimus IR)

Region → page path

The region part of some Awin page addresses ({loc}, e.g. us). Learned by itself when a region's pages load; correct or add pairs here. Without a pair, us is tried first. Saved as soon as you change it.

Review history

The last 30 reviews are kept in this browser, for the review window's History. Reviews still running are kept.

The extension counts each use (checklist fills, Moonpull scans, New Implementation and IR) by the record's Assigned TC, to measure adoption. No client information is recorded.
